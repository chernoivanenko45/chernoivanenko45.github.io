Do I need a powerful PC?

A modern Windows PC is enough for Standard and regular 1080p output. Deep AI and AI ×2 work best with 16 GB RAM and a discrete GPU.

Are my videos uploaded to the cloud?

No. Core processing runs locally. Internet is used for first activation, license checks, updates and optional Telegram integration.

Can I use my own music?

Yes. Add a separate track or keep the original audio from the source video.

What is the difference between Standard and Deep AI?

Standard is faster and produces a clean beat-driven edit. Deep AI performs deeper music, motion and scene analysis for more complex direction.

How do I receive the application after payment?

After payment confirmation, the site provides a license key and a secure installer link. Keep the key in a safe place.
